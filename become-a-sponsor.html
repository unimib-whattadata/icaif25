Become a Sponsor

Partner with ICAIF '26 to connect with leading researchers, practitioners, and innovators at the intersection of AI and Finance.

Why Sponsor ICAIF '26?

The ACM International Conference on AI in Finance (ICAIF) is a premier forum for sharing research and innovation. Sponsoring ICAIF '26 in Milan offers your organization unique opportunities to:

  • Recruit top talent: Connect with world-class researchers and highly skilled practitioners in AI and financial services.
  • Enhance brand visibility: Showcase your organization's commitment to innovation and thought leadership to a targeted, global audience.
  • Network with leaders: Build relationships with key decision-makers from academia, industry, and regulatory agencies.

À la Carte Sponsorship Opportunities

Enhance your brand's visibility with these targeted sponsorship options. These can be purchased individually or added to an existing sponsorship tier.

Best Paper Award Naming

€5,000

Naming: “Brand” ICAIF Best Paper Award. The award recognizes the best paper overall, with €3,000 provided to the winning authors. Requires a five-year commitment and Executive Committee approval.

Best Student Paper Award Naming

€4,000

Naming: “Brand” ICAIF Best Student Paper Award. The award recognizes the best paper led by a student, with €2,000 provided to the winning authors. Requires a five-year commitment and Executive Committee approval.

Workshop or Competition Sponsorship

€5,000

Naming: “Brand” workshop or competition sponsorship. Benefits, including possible student funding, are agreed with the relevant organizers.

Ten Sponsored Registrations

€5,000

Registrations are awarded in the sponsor’s name, for example as the “Brand” ICAIF Scholarship for selected attendees.

Conference Lanyard or Bag Branding

€3,000 each

Place your brand on conference lanyards or bags and associated conference merchandise.
