Call for Student Volunteers

Student Volunteer applications for ICAIF '26 are now closed. Thank you for your interest in joining the team in Milan, November 14–17, 2026.

Help make ICAIF '26 happen

The 7th ACM International Conference on AI in Finance invited applications for 30 Student Volunteer places.

Work alongside the organizing team and meet researchers and practitioners from academia, industry and the financial sector.

Applicants must be enrolled in a university program (Bachelor’s, Master’s or PhD) at the time of the conference.

Application dates

Application deadline
October 21, 2026, at 17:00
Milan time (CEST, UTC+2)
Announced notification date
October 23, 2026

Applications are closed.

The application deadline was October 21, 2026, at 17:00 Milan time (CEST).

What volunteers do

Student Volunteers help the organizing team make the conference run smoothly. Tasks include:

  • Registration desk support.
  • Session and room assistance.
  • Help to speakers and attendees.
  • Logistics during social events.

Each volunteer will be assigned a number of shifts across the conference days and is expected to attend a short briefing before the conference starts.

What volunteers receive

  • Free conference registration, giving access to all technical sessions, keynotes, workshops and tutorials.
  • An opportunity to meet researchers and practitioners from academia, industry and the financial sector.
  • A certificate of participation as an ICAIF 2026 Student Volunteer.

Travel and accommodation costs are not covered.

Volunteer registration cannot be used to cover an accepted paper. Main conference papers require an in-person Main Conference or Passport registration at the ACM member or non-member rate. For workshop papers, an in-person Workshop Days registration or a One Day registration for the day of the workshop is sufficient. See the author registration instructions.

Application requirements

Applications are now closed. The following documents and information were required:

  1. A CV, maximum 2 pages.
  2. A short statement of up to 300 words explaining why you would like to volunteer at ICAIF 2026.
  3. Proof of student status: a certificate of enrollment, or for PhD students a letter from the supervisor.
  4. Your availability on each conference day, November 14–17.
  5. The languages you speak.
  6. Whether you have previously served as a volunteer at a scientific conference, and if so, which one.
  7. Optionally, your gender, as you self-identify.

Applications are closed.

The application deadline was October 21, 2026, at 17:00 Milan time (CEST).

Selection

The call is for 30 Student Volunteer places. The announced selection criteria are:

  • Level of study (PhD, Master’s or Bachelor’s students).
  • Geographical origin.
  • Gender balance.

The call gives priority to students who have not previously served as volunteers at a scientific conference.

The announced selection notification date was October 23, 2026. For updates about your application, contact the organizing team using the address below.

Read the Student Volunteer application privacy information for details about how application data is handled.

For any questions, please contact info@icaif26.org.
