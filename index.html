Research at ICAIF '26

Nine areas, organized into three connected themes spanning methods, responsible deployment, markets, and societal impact.

Emerging AI methods

  • Generative AI & foundation models Simulation, synthetic data, and financial decision support.
  • AI agents & reinforcement learning Multi-agent systems and sequential decision-making.
  • Natural language & multimodal analysis Text, images, time series, and audio in finance.

Trust, risk & customers

  • Trustworthy & responsible AI Explainability, fairness, privacy, and governance.
  • Risk management & fraud detection Robustness, validation, and uncertainty quantification.
  • Credit & customer analytics Credit decisions, behavior analysis, and default detection.

Markets & societal impact

  • Trading & asset management Asset pricing, investment strategies, and time-series analysis.
  • Blockchain & decentralized finance Cryptocurrency, federated learning, and DeFi applications.
  • Financial inclusion & sustainable investing Environmental, social, and governance (ESG) applications.

The Host City

Welcome to Milan

Milan is Italy's financial center and a rapidly growing hub for European technology. The conference will be held at Bocconi University, one of Europe's leading institutions for economics, management, and finance.

  • Venue: Bocconi
  • Airports: Malpensa (MXP), Linate (LIN), Bergamo (BGY)
