Programme releases, deadline updates and other announcements, newest first.

October 2026

  1. 24 October 2026

    Student volunteer applications closed

    The call for ICAIF 2026 Student Volunteers has closed. The application deadline was October 21, 2026, at 17:00 Milan time (CEST, UTC+2), and the application form is no longer accepting responses. Thank you to everyone who applied.

    View the volunteer call
  2. 24 October 2026

    Meet the ICAIF '26 keynote speakers

    Daniele Magazzeni and Michael Barr are announced for November 16; Mauro Micillo, Raffaele Savi and Stefano Pasquali join us on November 17. Meet the speakers on the homepage and consult the preliminary programme for scheduled sessions. Michael Barr’s session time and room are still to be confirmed.

    Meet the keynote speakers
  3. 19 October 2026

    Student volunteer applications opened

    Applications opened for the team of 30 student volunteers in Milan on November 14–17, with a deadline of October 21, 2026, at 17:00 Milan time (CEST, UTC+2). Applications are now closed; see the October 24 closure update.

    View the volunteer call
  4. 17 October 2026

    Call for Student Volunteers

    The call invited applications for 30 student volunteer places at ICAIF '26 in Milan on November 14–17, with free conference registration and a certificate of participation. Applications closed after the October 21, 2026, 17:00 Milan-time deadline. See the October 24 closure update.

    Read the volunteer call
  5. 17 October 2026

    Workshop paper author registration deadline extended

    Only authors of accepted workshop papers have an extended registration deadline of October 23, 2026, instead of October 18. All other authors must register by October 18, 2026. Registration deadlines are at 23:59 AoE (Anywhere on Earth). The workshop paper notification deadline remains October 16, 2026.

    View registration dates
  6. 17 October 2026

    Travel grant applications closed

    The application deadline was October 15, 2026. The shared form for travel grants and QRT Student Travel Awards is no longer accepting responses.

    View travel grant details
  7. 16 October 2026

    Needlestack Technologies joins as an "à la carte sponsor"

  8. 14 October 2026

    Travel grant application deadline reminder

    The application deadline was October 15, 2026. Applications are now closed.

    View travel grant details
  9. 8 October 2026

    Tutorial details published

    The four tutorials cover document intelligence, Bayesian calibration, confidence and calibration for financial LLMs, and financial agents. Each listing includes an abstract, presenter details and scheduled times.

    Explore the tutorials
  10. 8 October 2026

    Preliminary programme published

    Browse the November 14–17 schedule by day, room and session type, and save your personal agenda. Details remain subject to updates.

    Explore the programme
  11. 1 October 2026

    Workshop paper deadlines extended

    Workshop paper submissions were extended to October 12, 2026, instead of October 1. Acceptance notifications moved from October 15 to October 16. The author registration deadline announced then was October 18. Deadlines are at 23:59 AoE.

    Update (October 17): registration is now due October 23, 2026, only for authors of accepted workshop papers. All other authors must register by October 18. See the latest registration announcement.

    View workshop information
