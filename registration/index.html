Important Note to Authors

  • Each accepted main conference paper must be covered by at least one in-person registration, either Main Conference or Passport Registration, at the ACM member or non-member rate.
  • The presenting author is required to travel to Milan and attend in person to present the paper, either orally or as a poster.
  • Participants can choose either in-person or virtual attendance, subject to the author registration requirement above.
  • Each accepted paper must have its author registration completed by October 18, 2026, 23:59 AoE (Anywhere on Earth), so the presentation schedule can be arranged.

Registration Entitlements

In-Person Registrations

  • Access to conference sessions and the exhibition hall on the days covered by your registration.
  • Daily coffee breaks and lunch. Breakfast and dinners are not included.
  • Passport registration includes all four conference days, November 14-17, 2026.
  • Main Conference registration covers November 16-17, 2026.
  • Passport and Main Conference registrations include admission to the Welcome Reception on November 15 and the Conference Banquet on November 16.
  • Workshop and One-Day registrations do not include these social events, but tickets may be purchased separately.

Virtual Registration

Virtual registration covers all four days, November 14-17, 2026, with access to live-streamed sessions through the online platform.

Applications Open

ICAIF 2026 Travel Grant Application

Travel grant applications are open to students and early-career researchers who require financial support to attend ICAIF 2026. Applicants are not required to have submitted a paper or other contribution to the conference. However, active involvement in ICAIF 2026, including a submitted or accepted paper, workshop contribution, poster, demonstration, volunteering role, or other relevant participation, may be considered positively during the selection process.

Students can also read about the QRT Student Travel Awards, including eligibility and selection information.

Submit your application

Complete the travel grant application using the secure Google Form.

Open the application

The Google Form opens in a new tab.

The application is hosted on Google Forms and was created within the University of Milano-Bicocca. Review the privacy information before submitting personal data.

How applications are assessed

Applications will be assessed based on financial need, the expected academic or professional benefit of attending ICAIF, the applicant's involvement in the conference, and the objective of supporting broad geographic and institutional participation.

VAT Information

The fees shown above exclude 22% VAT. The registration form shows the VAT-inclusive amount where VAT applies. VAT treatment depends on the invoice holder and payment details provided during registration.

When VAT Applies

  • VAT is due when the invoice is issued to an individual acting as a private person, whether Italian, EU, or non-EU.
  • VAT is due when the invoice is issued to an Italian university, through electronic invoicing.
  • If the invoice is issued to a university outside Italy, VAT is not required. For EU universities, a VAT registration number will be required.

VAT Exemption

Participants from EU countries excluding Italy, and from non-EU countries, should select the VAT exemption option during registration when one of the following applies:

  • The registration fee is paid with the university's credit card, not a personal card.
  • An advance invoice is required for the university and will be paid by the university via bank transfer.

Universities and companies in EU countries excluding Italy, and in non-EU countries, are subject to reverse charge rules. In those cases, the registration fee must be invoiced without VAT when the conditions above apply.

Cancellation Policy

In line with ACM's standard policy, registration fees are generally non-refundable.

The only exception applies to participants whose visa applications are denied. In such cases, a refund will be issued minus a processing fee.

Support

If you require a visa support letter, please complete your registration first. For assistance, use the registration contact below.

If you experience problems during registration, contact eventi@consultaumbria.com or congressi@consultaumbria.com.
