# Public sources for indoor wayfinding

Checked on 30 October 2026. The three organiser-supplied artwork-layout PDFs supply the detailed floor geometry; the published architectural plans corroborate the building layout and circulation. These sources do not establish ICAIF room assignments or conference access arrangements.

## Programme room names

The organiser confirmed in this chat on 30 October 2026 that Main Hall is Aula Magna (Level −1), and Room 1, Room 2 and Room 3 correspond respectively to AS01, AS02 and AS03 (Level −2). The programme's Foyer link selects the Level −1 foyer beside Main Hall. `programmeRooms` in `data/venue-floors.json` is the shared assignment source for map labels and programme links; stable physical place IDs are preserved. AS02 also retains its Deutsche Bank Room alias. Conference entrance and registration arrangements remain unconfirmed.

## Street-side registration

- [Bocconi Art Gallery 2024 catalogue, PDF page 6](https://www.unibocconi.it/sites/default/files/media/attachments/060%2024_Catalogo%20BAG_LR%20%281%29.pdf) provides an official campus map with named streets and the Röntgen/Velodromo footprints.
- [Published architecture dossier, PDF pages 12–13](https://www.architetturadipietra.it/wp/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/bocconi2.pdf) contains the matching site and floor plans.
- [Architect-provided project description on ArchDaily](https://www.archdaily.com/874794/universita-luigi-bocconi-grafton-architects) identifies the Aula Magna frontage with the northern Viale Bligny edge.

Comparing these diagrams registers the left sloping edge of the indoor drawings to Viale Bligny, and the long bottom edge to Via Guglielmo Röntgen. This is a source-derived registration, rather than a north-up assumption. All three maps retain their shared drawing orientation. Street labels indicate building sides; no new doors or route arrows are inferred.

## Vertical circulation

The revised redraw uses a common scale and registration for the three source sheets, anchored on repeated shafts and service cores. Each staircase retains its individual runs and landings instead of being reduced to one generic rectangle. The lift shafts are distinguished from adjacent toilet cubicles and technical shafts; the compact foyer core also contains a return staircase that was missing from the earlier redraw.

Repeated plan geometry and the source stair identifiers establish these correspondences:

| Core | Floors represented | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lift behind Aula Magna | Ground, −1, −2 | Repeated shaft and surrounding return stair; the basement sheets label the lift 600 kg. |
| Compact foyer return stair | Ground, −1, −2 | S7 appears in the same core on all three sheets, alongside the foyer shaft and toilet cubicles. |
| Broad stairs beside Aula Magna | Ground, −1 | Matching runs and landings; the −1 plan labels this S41. The corresponding −2 space is not a continuation. |
| Stair beside the toilet block | −1, −2 | Both basement sheets label this separate core S30. It is distinct from S41 and the broad central-right staircase. |

These identifiers are source references, not proposed signage. Selected stairs and lifts can link to the corresponding core on another mapped floor; changing floors preserves the zoom and highlights the matching location. A geometric correspondence is not a verified step-free route, a statement of public lift access, or a conference itinerary. Unresolved stair continuations receive no inferred destination.

## Names and practical landmarks

- [Official Röntgen brochure, May 2008, PDF pages 4 and 6](https://www.unibocconi.it/sites/default/files/media/attachments/brochure_GRAFTON%2821x21%294_4.pdf): Aula Magna entrance frontage faces Viale Bligny; a photograph of the floor directory uses 0 for the ground floor. The map summary now includes Floor 0.
- [Bocconi Buildings directory](https://www.unibocconi.it/en/campus/buildings-and-classrooms/buildings): AS02 is also called Deutsche Bank Room. Keep AS02 on the map and in stable IDs; expose the alias in the directory, quick selector and selected-place details.
- [Drink Sustainably](https://www.unibocconi.it/en/about-us/sustainability/drink-sustainably): water refill on level −1 near the lifts, and on level −2 in front of AS01–AS03. These are descriptive locations, not surveyed coordinates, so no exact dispenser pin is drawn.
- [Life in Milan and Life on Campus](https://www.unibocconi.it/en/programs/phd/life-milan-and-life-campus): GUD Milano Bocconi is on the ground floor of the Röntgen building. It is a descriptive landmark; the page does not assert opening during conference weekends.
- [Official Classrooms directory](https://www.unibocconi.it/en/campus/buildings-and-classrooms/classrooms): AS04–AS05 are on the second basement level. The water-refill guide independently places AS01–AS03 on level −2, corroborating the supplied plan.
